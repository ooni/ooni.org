Are you interested in city-level information in OONI data? Would you like to
contribute additional geographic and network-level data through OONI Probe?
We’d love to hear your feedback!

We released a **[new OONI Probe TLS Middlebox experiment](https://ooni.org/post/2026-ooni-probe-tls-middlebox-experiment)** through which you can help
contribute measurements to identify the locations of censorship devices around
the world. While the advanced mode of this experiment offers great research
opportunities, it also introduces new potential risks.

We are therefore seeking community feedback on how comfortable you would feel
with us collecting additional geographic and network-level data, and how useful
you would find this information.

Please **take a few minutes to complete our survey** and share your feedback: https://forms.gle/z5kjVTTUGLryRFcK8 

Please feel free to share the survey with other OONI Probe users, as well as
with those who use OONI measurements in their work. The survey will remain open
for responses until **December 10, 2026**.

Thank you for participating! Your input is very valuable to us and greatly
appreciated. Your feedback will help us advance OONI’s censorship measurement
methodologies to better meet the needs of the global internet freedom
community.

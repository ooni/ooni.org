Unbiased information for free minds – that is the Deutsche Welle (DW) brand promise. As Germany’s international broadcaster and an independent media outlet, DW provides unbiased news and information in more than 30 languages around the world so that people can form their own opinions. DW’s TV, online and radio services reach 337 million users every week.

In some of DW's target regions, its content is [blocked](https://corporate.dw.com/en/dw-internet-censorship-monitor/a-79672759) due to local laws and restrictions. To monitor this continuously, DW has been partnering with OONI for years and closely tracks which countries are blocking the service. DW provides circumvention tools and guidance through its [Access Unlocked](https://www.access-unlocked.org/en/index.html) page.
